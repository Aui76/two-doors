Two doors

A museum where you stand inside a real hack at the block before it happened and find the loophole yourself. Then you have two doors. One is the attack. The other is filing what you found on DAN, and DAN pays you for it.

Every room is a Foundry test on a fork of a public chain at a named block. I typed none of the numbers on the walls. The tests read them off the chain and print them, and each README puts the command next to the number.
